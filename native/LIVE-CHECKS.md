# String 2.0 live checks

Play two songs in queue order. Keep the volume low for the output check.

- **Keyboard or headset.** With another window in front, press Play/Pause twice, then Next and immediately Previous. String should pause, resume and move one song per press while the other window keeps focus.
- **Windows media controls.** Open the Windows media panel and select String. Check the song title, artist, cover and play state. Press Next in the panel. The new song and its artwork should appear in both places.
- **Taskbar preview.** Hover over String on the taskbar. Click the middle button twice. Its icon and tooltip should switch between Play and Pause. Click Next and immediately Previous. Each should move one song.
- **Discord profile.** With activity sharing enabled and your application ID set in String, play a song and look at your profile. The Listening activity should show your selected display and a moving time bar. Pause and allow one update interval (about five seconds) for it to clear.
- **Audio output.** While a song plays, switch the default output to another device. Listen for the same song continuing near the same position on that device. Unplug the active device. Playback should move to the fallback, or wait at its position until you reconnect an output. Repeat the switch while paused and confirm it stays silent.

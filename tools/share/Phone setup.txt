YOUR MUSIC ON YOUR PHONE
========================

String itself only runs on Windows. For your phone, three free programs do the job:

  - Navidrome, on your PC: a small music server that shares your music folder.
  - Tailscale, on your PC and phone: a private link between the two. It works anywhere
    your phone has internet, and only your own devices are on it.
  - A music app on the phone: Amperfy on iPhone (free). On Android, Symfonium or Ultrasonic.

Your songs come across with their names and covers. String's likes and playlists don't
(the phone app keeps its own). What you play on the phone still counts in String's Stats.

It takes about 15 minutes. Do it at home, with the PC on.


ON THE PC
---------
1. Install Navidrome
   - Go to  https://github.com/navidrome/navidrome/releases/latest
   - Under "Assets", download the file that ends in  _windows_amd64_installer.msi , then
     open it.
   - When it asks for the music folder, replace C:\Music with the folder your songs are in,
     for example  C:\Users\YOURNAME\Music . Leave the port at 4533.
   - Click Yes when Windows asks to allow changes. Navidrome then runs in the background
     and starts with Windows.

2. Make your Navidrome login
   - On the PC, open  http://localhost:4533  in your browser.
   - The first time, it asks you to make a username and password. Your phone will use
     this login, so remember it.

3. Install Tailscale
   - Download it from  https://tailscale.com/download  (Windows), install it, and sign in
     with Google, Microsoft, Apple or GitHub. It's free for personal use.
   - Find your PC's Tailscale name: click the Tailscale icon by the clock (it may be
     hidden under the ^ arrow), or open  https://login.tailscale.com/admin/machines .
     It's usually your PC's name in small letters. These steps call it PCNAME.

4. Let your phone in (one command)
   - Click Start, type  PowerShell , right-click "Windows PowerShell" and choose
     "Run as administrator".
   - Paste this whole line and press Enter:

New-NetFirewallRule -DisplayName "Navidrome (Tailscale only)" -Direction Inbound -Protocol TCP -LocalPort 4533 -RemoteAddress 100.64.0.0/10 -Action Allow

   - This lets in only addresses from Tailscale's range (100.64.x.x to 100.127.x.x),
     which is where your phone shows up while Tailscale is on. Home Wi-Fi and the
     internet use other addresses. Don't forward any ports on your router: you don't
     need to, and it would open Navidrome to the internet.


ON AN IPHONE
------------
5. Install "Tailscale" from the App Store. Sign in with the SAME account as on the PC,
   allow it to add a VPN configuration, and switch it on.
   (iPhones list it as a VPN. It only carries traffic between your own devices; your
   normal browsing and apps don't go through it.)

6. Install "Amperfy Music" from the App Store (free; its description mentions Ampache
   and Subsonic). Log in with:
     Server:    http://PCNAME:4533      (with http:// at the start, not https)
     Username and password: your Navidrome login from step 2

   Quick check if it won't connect: in Safari, open  http://PCNAME:4533 . You should see
   Navidrome's page. Type the http:// part, or Safari searches Google instead.

7. Download songs to play without internet (Amperfy calls this "caching"):
   - Library tab > Albums, Artists or Playlists > press and hold one > Download.
   - Downloaded songs show "Cached". They play anywhere, even with the PC off.


ON ANDROID
----------
Same idea: install Tailscale from the Play Store and sign in with the same account. Then
get a music app that works with Navidrome (a "Subsonic" app), such as Symfonium (paid,
very good) or Ultrasonic (free). Server  http://PCNAME:4533 , and your Navidrome login.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Streaming needs the PC on and awake. While the PC sleeps or is off, only downloaded
  songs play.
- After the PC restarts, Tailscale connects once you sign in to Windows. To have it
  connect even before you sign in, right-click the Tailscale icon and turn on
  "Run unattended" (under Preferences).
- Distance doesn't matter: it works anywhere your phone has internet, on Wi-Fi or mobile
  data. Streaming uses roughly 1 MB of data per minute.
- With Tailscale switched off on the phone, only downloaded songs play.
- New songs show up on the phone by themselves a few minutes after they land in your
  music folder. In Amperfy, pull down on the library to refresh it.
- Phone plays appear in String's Stats as "on your phone": String picks them up shortly
  after it opens, and every few minutes while it's open. A play counts once the phone
  app reports it to Navidrome, which apps do partway through a song or at its end, so
  quick skips usually don't count. If you make more than one Navidrome account, pick
  yours on String's Stats page first.
